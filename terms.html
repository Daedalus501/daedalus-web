9. Emergency features — please read this carefully

Daedalus includes an SOS function and emergency contact handling. These are convenience features layered on top of your own emergency planning. They are not an emergency service.

  • We do not contact the emergency services on your behalf, and we are not notified when you use SOS. The SOS function prepares a message to the emergency contacts you have configured, and offers a shortcut that dials 112 from your own phone. Whether that message is delivered, and whether anyone reads or acts on it, is outside our control.
  • SOS requires mobile coverage. With no coverage, no message is sent. Where the app indicates that an SOS has been queued for later delivery, that means only that the app will attempt to record it when connectivity returns. It does not mean that anyone has been alerted.
  • In an emergency, call 112 (or the local emergency number) first. Never delay contacting the emergency services in order to use the app.
  • Position data may be missing, delayed, or inaccurate. GPS depends on the device, satellite visibility, battery level, operating-system permissions, and power-saving behaviour. Gaps in a recorded route are expected and are not a malfunction.
  • Weather and avalanche information is supplied by third parties, currently the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (api.met.no) and NVE / Varsom (api01.nve.no). It may be delayed, incomplete, or unavailable. It is provided for information only, is never a substitute for the official forecast at varsom.no and yr.no, and is never a substitute for your own assessment in the field.
  • The web command centre is not a staffed monitoring service. It shows your organization's own data to your organization's own people. Nobody at Høi Z ENK is watching it.
